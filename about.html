My Table Philosophy

Playstyle & Mechanics

Balance: I love a healthy mix of roleplay and combat, adapting to whatever needs the players have.

The Rules: Necessary, but occasionally taken with a grain of salt if breaking them benefits the story or the "Rule of Cool."

Min/Maxing: I advise against it. Balancing encounters for a min/maxed player puts the rest of the party at risk, and keeping things easy makes the game boring.

The Ideal Table

Engagement: I love players who are engaged in the narrative and provide their own unique take on the world.

The Spotlight: I strive to give everyone a fair shot at the spotlight. Truly cinematic moments happen when players respect each other's moments without trying to hijack them.

Creativity: Have an insane plan and hope the dice roll in your favor? I love it. Succeeding or failing is just part of our unique story.
