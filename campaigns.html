📜 How Booking a New Campaign Works

You are booking a seat for a brand new adventure! Because D&D is a cooperative game, we need a "Minimum Viable Party" to ensure the game is actually fun for you.

  • Gathering the Party: The campaign officially launches as soon as we reach 3 out of 5 players. This typically takes 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Instant Access: Upon subscribing, you will immediately get access to our private Discord server to start chatting, building your character, and meeting me!
  • Zero Risk: If the table takes longer than expected to fill, you are never trapped. You can withdraw your seat for a full, hassle-free refund at any time before Session 0 begins.
  • Bring a Friend Bonus: Don't want to wait? If you bring a friend to the table, you both get 25% off your first session! Just let me know in the Discord.
